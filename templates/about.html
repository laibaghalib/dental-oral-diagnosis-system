Our Story

Why We Built This

Dental disease is one of the most widespread health problems in Pakistan, yet millions go undiagnosed simply because dental visits are expensive, inconvenient, or unavailable in rural areas.

DentalAI was built to change that. Using a 3-model AI ensemble trained on thousands of real dental images, we give anyone with a smartphone the ability to screen for 6 common dental conditions — instantly, for free.

Our goal is simple: catch dental problems early, before they become painful and expensive.

The Technology

Three Models. One Diagnosis.

DentalAI combines three state-of-the-art deep learning models into an ensemble — each specialising in different visual patterns, together achieving higher accuracy than any single model alone.

🧠
Model 1

ResNet18

A residual network that excels at learning deep visual features. Strong at detecting structural dental abnormalities like cavities and hypodontia.

⚡
Model 2

DenseNet121

Dense connections between layers enable rich feature reuse. Particularly effective at detecting subtle texture changes like early calculus and discoloration.

🔬
Model 3

EfficientNetV2

The most efficient and accurate of the three. Scales depth, width and resolution together for superior performance on complex dental conditions like gingivitis and ulcers.

What We Detect

6 Dental Conditions

Our AI is trained to detect these conditions from a single dental photo.

🦠

Dental Caries

The most common dental disease worldwide. AI detects early-stage cavities before they spread deeper into the tooth.

🪨

Calculus

Hardened plaque (tartar) that damages gums over time. Early detection allows professional cleaning before damage sets in.

🔴

Gingivitis

Inflamed, red and bleeding gums — the earliest stage of gum disease. Fully reversible when caught early.

⚠️

Oral Ulcers

Painful sores on the inner mouth. AI identifies ulcer type and severity to recommend the right treatment plan.

🌗

Tooth Discoloration

AI identifies whether discoloration is surface staining or deeper structural — guiding appropriate treatment.

🦷

Hypodontia

Congenitally missing teeth. Early diagnosis helps plan implants, bridges or orthodontic treatment effectively.
